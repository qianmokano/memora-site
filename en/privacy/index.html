Memora Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

中文

Overview

Memora is a study app for the Chinese graduate entrance exam. The developer does not collect or upload any of your data, and cannot see it. Memora has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools.

Your data only goes to three places: your device, the AI provider you choose, and the sync you turn on yourself (iCloud or WebDAV). Each is described below.

Data stored on your device

Conversations, mistakes, question photos, review history, study plans, your learner profile, what the AI remembers about your studies, and focus sessions are all stored on your device.

What is sent to your AI provider

Memora has no built-in AI. In Settings → AI Services you connect your own account with a provider such as DeepSeek, Qwen, Zhipu, Kimi, OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, or another address you enter.

When you ask a question, photograph a problem, organize mistakes, have work graded, generate practice, or get review suggestions and study reports, the app sends what that task needs directly to that provider:

  • the question text and images, and the current conversation;
  • related mistakes, your learner profile (such as subjects and exam date), and what the AI remembers about your studies.

These requests use your own API key and go straight from your device, never through a server of the developer. How the provider handles them is governed by its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent until you connect an AI service.

API keys

API keys are stored in your device's keychain. With iCloud sync on, they sync between your devices through iCloud Keychain.

WebDAV sync also contains your API keys, unencrypted. When you export a backup, you choose whether its keys are encrypted with a password (the default), left unencrypted, or left out.

Sync

Sync is off by default. You can turn it on in Settings → Sync.

  • iCloud: data is kept in your own private iCloud storage, readable only by devices signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • WebDAV: data is kept on the server you enter, such as Jianguoyun or your own NAS.

The developer has no access to either.

Backups

You export backups yourself in Settings → Backup & Restore and keep them wherever you choose. Apart from API keys, which can be encrypted, the contents of a backup are not encrypted.

Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph questions.
  • Photos: through the system photo picker, so the app only sees the photos you pick.
  • Notifications: review and focus reminders, created on your device.
  • Screen Time: for Forced Focus, which blocks the apps you choose during a focus session. The system keeps your choice; Memora only receives opaque tokens and cannot tell which apps they are.

Logs and crash reports

The app records events and crash reports on your device to help fix problems. Logs say what happened and how many times, never questions, conversations or API keys, and keep only the last 7 days.

They are never uploaded automatically. They leave your device only if you export them in Settings → Developer → Logs and send them to the developer. If you turned on "Share with App Developers" in system settings, Apple passes crash data to the developer under its own policy.

Deleting your data

  • Deleting the app deletes all data on your device.
  • Data in iCloud can be deleted in Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage.
  • Data on WebDAV can be deleted on your server.
  • Content sent to an AI provider is handled under that provider's terms.

Children

Memora is meant for people preparing for the graduate entrance exam, not for children under 13, and does not knowingly collect anyone's personal information.

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, this policy and the effective date at the top will be updated.
