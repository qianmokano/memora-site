Memora Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

中文

1. Introduction

Memora (the "App") is a study app for the National Postgraduate Entrance Examination of China. This policy explains how the App handles your information. By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

The developer of the App ("we", "us") does not collect, store, or have access to any of your personal information or usage data. The App has no user accounts, displays no advertising, contains no analytics, measurement or tracking tools, and does not rely on any server operated by us.

Your information may reside only in the following places: your device; the artificial intelligence service provider you choose; and any sync service you choose to enable (iCloud or WebDAV).

2. Information stored on your device

Conversations, mistakes, question images, review history, study plans, your learner profile, study notes kept by the AI, and focus records created in the App are stored on your device.

3. Information sent to AI service providers

The App does not include a built-in artificial intelligence service. You configure a provider yourself in Settings → AI Services, such as DeepSeek, Qwen, Zhipu, Kimi, OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, or another service address you enter.

When you use features such as asking questions, photographing problems, organizing mistakes, grading, practice generation, review recommendations or study reports, the App sends the following information needed for that feature to the provider you configured:

  • question text, question images and the current conversation;
  • related mistakes, your learner profile (such as subjects and exam date), and study notes kept by the AI.

These requests use your own API key and are sent directly from your device to the provider, without passing through any server of ours. The provider's handling of this information is governed by its own privacy policy. The App sends no information before you configure a service.

4. API keys

Your API keys are stored in your device's keychain. When iCloud sync is enabled, they are synced between your devices through iCloud Keychain.

Data synced via WebDAV includes your API keys. When exporting a backup, you may choose to encrypt your API keys with a password (the default), not to encrypt them, or to exclude them. Please keep your sync account and backup files secure.

5. Sync

Sync is disabled by default and may be enabled in Settings → Sync:

  • iCloud: data is stored in your own private iCloud storage, accessible only to devices signed in to the same Apple Account, and is protected under Apple's applicable policies.
  • WebDAV: data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored on the server you specify (for example Jianguoyun or your own network storage); its protection depends on that server.

We have no access to data in either location.

6. Backups

Backup files are exported by you in Settings → Backup & Restore, stored in a location of your choice, and kept by you. Protection of API keys in backups is described in Section 4.

7. System permissions

The App requests the following permissions only when you use the related feature. You may revoke them at any time in system settings:

  • Camera: to photograph questions.
  • Photos: images are chosen through the system photo picker, and the App can read only the images you select.
  • Notifications: for review and focus reminders, generated locally on your device.
  • Screen Time: for the Forced Focus feature, which restricts the apps you select during a focus session. Your selection is held by the system; the App receives only identifiers that do not reveal which apps they are.

8. Logs and crash reports

The App records operational events and crash reports on your device to keep it running reliably. Logs record only event types and counts, never questions, conversation content or API keys, and are kept for the last 7 days only. Logs and crash reports remain on your device and are not uploaded.

If you have enabled "Share with App Developers" in system settings, or if you take part in testing through TestFlight, Apple may provide us with crash reports and any feedback you choose to submit, in accordance with Apple's policies.

9. Deleting your information

  • Deleting the App deletes all information stored on your device.
  • Data in iCloud can be deleted in Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage.
  • Data on WebDAV can be deleted on your server.
  • Information already sent to an AI service provider is handled under that provider's terms.

10. Children

The App is intended for people preparing for postgraduate entrance examinations and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

11. Changes to this policy

If the way the App handles information changes, we will update this policy and the effective date at the top of this page.
